Privacy Policy

How Kijarney collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kijarney. Kijarney is a data science and data analysis platform that helps users clean, analyze, visualize and work with datasets.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use Kijarney.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kijarney, we may collect information such as:

  • Name,profile,location and email address associated with your account.
  • Profile information provided through authentication providers such as Google or Facebook.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Datasets and files that you choose to upload to Kijarney.
  • Information about how you use features of the platform.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

3. Information From Google and Facebook

If you choose to sign in using Google or Facebook, Kijarney may receive information permitted by the authentication provider, such as your name, email address and basic profile information.

Kijarney only requests information that is necessary for account authentication and operation of the service.

4. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your Kijarney account.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide data analysis and data science functionality.
  • Process datasets and files uploaded by users.
  • Maintain and improve Kijarney.
  • Detect abuse, fraud and security problems.
  • Communicate with users about their accounts or the service.

5. Uploaded Datasets

Kijarney allows users to upload datasets for analysis. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to upload and process the data they submit.

You should not upload sensitive personal information or information that you are not authorized to process.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored and processed through Kijarney.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Sharing

Kijarney does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure providers where necessary to operate, maintain or secure Kijarney, subject to applicable agreements and requirements.

8. Authentication Providers

If you use Google or Facebook to authenticate with Kijarney, authentication is also subject to the respective provider's privacy policies and terms.

9. Cookies and Sessions

Kijarney may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain user sessions, remember authentication state and provide essential functionality.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of your information.

To request deletion of your Kijarney account and associated personal information, visit our Data Deletion page.

11. Children's Privacy

Kijarney is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Kijarney handles information, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Kijarney website.
