Data Deletion

Kijarney respects your right to control your personal information. You may request deletion of your Kijarney account and associated personal information.

Want your account deleted?
Contact Kijarney through the official contact information provided on our website and request account and data deletion.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Contact Kijarney using our official contact information.
  2. State that you want your Kijarney account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. Provide the email address associated with your Kijarney account.
  4. We may verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, account deletion may include:

  • Your Kijarney account.
  • Personal profile information.
  • Authentication information associated with your account.
  • User-uploaded datasets associated with your account.
  • Other personal information associated with your account.

Information We May Need to Retain

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate legal obligations.

Facebook Login Users

If you created or accessed your Kijarney account using Facebook Login, requesting deletion from Kijarney will remove the information associated with your Kijarney account that Kijarney is permitted and required to delete.

Removing Kijarney data does not automatically delete your Facebook account. You must manage your Facebook account separately through Meta if you wish to remove information held by Facebook.

Questions

For questions about data deletion, please contact Kijarney through the contact information available on our website.
